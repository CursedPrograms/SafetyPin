SAFETY PIN

electric violin distortion

A fat, bassy fuzz made for electric violin.

Turn it into a cello. Add a sub two octaves down. Throw it in the basement. Tune up without leaving the plugin.

BUILT-IN TUNER

Standard violin tuning: G D A E, A = 440 Hz. It listens to your clean signal, before any fuzz, octave or reverb, so the effects never confuse it.

Note, cents and the nearest string. Goes green when you're in tune. Click it to mute the output while you tune.

PLUG IN

Standalone

  1. Unzip, run Safety Pin.exe.
  2. Options > Audio/MIDI Settings: ASIO, your interface (e.g. Focusrite USB ASIO), the input your violin is in, buffer 64-128.
  3. Click Unmute Input. Headphones or an amp, not a mic near the speakers.

VST3

  1. Copy Safety Pin.vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3.
  2. Rescan plugins in your DAW (Cubase, Reaper, Ableton...).
  3. Put it on a mono track. It comes out stereo.

"Windows protected your PC"?

The files aren't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info > Run anyway. Only download it from this page or the GitHub release.

UNDER THE HOOD

  • Octave down: two read heads sweep a delay line at half speed; each sweep is a whole number of the note's periods, so the splices land in phase. No warble.
  • Sub bass: a flip-flop divider like the old analog octave pedals.
  • Fuzz: bow scratch cut and low mids pushed before the clipper, 4x oversampled.
  • Reverb: Jon Dattorro's plate, damped darker for strings.
  • Tuner: YIN pitch detection.

Works on guitar and viola too, but the octave and tuner only track from about 150 Hz up, and one note at a time.
